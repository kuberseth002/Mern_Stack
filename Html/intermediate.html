My recipe for the Best Cinnamons Rolls

Ingredients

For the dough:

  • 3/4 cup warm milk
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons yeast
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 egg plus 1 egg york
  • 3 cups bread flour

For the filling:

  • 3/4 cup dark brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoons ground Cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the milk with the yeast,sugar,eggs
  2. Melt the butter and add to the mixture
  3. Add in the flour and mix untill combined into a dough
  4. Knead the dough for 10 minutes
